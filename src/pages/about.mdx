# About Me

Hello! I'm **Mridul Gupta**, but you can call me EmGiXIII (pronounced "M G 13"). Welcome to my personal corner of the internet.

I'm a physics graduate who has recently embarked on a new and exciting chapter: a Ph.D. in Computer Science at the **Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi**. My research is focused on the fascinating world of **Quantum Computing**. This website serves as a living document of my academic explorations, creative projects, and everything in between.

You can view my full resume [here](/Resume.pdf).

## What is EmGiCO?

**EmGiCO** (pronounced "M G Co") is a childhood dream of mine, a name I came up with in 4th grade for a future company. For now, it's the banner under which I share my thoughts, projects, and discoveries on this blog. It stands for "Mridul Gupta and Company," and you're welcome to be a part of it.

## My Journey

### Academic Path

I graduated from the **Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Kolkata** with a **BS-MS Dual Degree** in Physics. My master's thesis explored an interesting intersection of number theory and computer science, focusing on converting prime factorization into a Boolean Satisfiability (B-SAT) problem.

This fall, I began my Ph.D. in Computer Science at **IIT Delhi**. I'm diving deep into quantum computation, with the goal of developing and analyzing novel algorithms that harness the power of quantum mechanics.

### Skills & Passions

My technical toolkit includes **Python, TypeScript, and Rust**. I'm comfortable with scientific computing libraries like **NumPy** and **Matplotlib**, and I enjoy building web experiences with frameworks like **React** and **Astro**.

Outside of academia, I'm passionate about **generative and digital art**. I also have a soft spot for the intellectual challenge of esoteric programming languages like **Brainf*ck**.
